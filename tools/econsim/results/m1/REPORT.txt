12. Balance failures and bottlenecks found by the model
====================================================================================================
 - Frostbound Reach (legacy Frost density 1/150) pays less than dense Crystal Wilds at every horizon: players stay in Crystal Wilds even after unlocking Frostbound (audit M8). Must be fixed by the Frostbound build density/rewards (M4b): see section 9.
 - Free players do not reach a Divine weapon by rolling within 200 h at median (Divine gamma 0.5). Deterministic Divine route (forge, M2/M6) is still required.
